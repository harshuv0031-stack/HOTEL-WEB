VELORA HOTEL — UI WEBSITE
================================
Open index.html to start.

Files:
- index.html       Home
- rooms.html       Rooms & suites
- experience.html  Experiences
- gallery.html     Gallery
- story.html       Story
- contact.html     Contact form (UI demo)
- booking.html     Booking form (UI demo)
- style.css        Complete responsive styling
- script.js        Animations, mobile menu, forms and interactions

IMPORTANT:
This is a frontend/UI project. Contact and booking forms show a success message but do not send/store data.
Images are loaded from Unsplash over the internet, and Google Fonts are loaded remotely.
For production use, replace those with your own optimized local images and connect forms to a backend.
